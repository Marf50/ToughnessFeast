ToughnessFeast
==============

Hover HUNGER. That is the Feast journal.

  Feast          Hiver  toughness 23
  Food           248 / 300
  Status         ON  heal speed 83% of full
  Left Arm       GONE  bud 34%  ~18 min
  Right Arm      62%  healing  ~8 min
  Left Leg       OK  100%
  Chest          88%  healing

Hurt limbs show the same % as the HUD bars (not a fake 100%).
Missing limbs show bud/grow % and time to the next stage.
Limbs only regrow when food is over 200.

Extract this folder into Kenshi/mods/ so you have:

  Kenshi/mods/ToughnessFeast/ToughnessFeast.dll
  Kenshi/mods/ToughnessFeast/config.ini
  ...

Enable the mod in the launcher. Needs RE_Kenshi.
