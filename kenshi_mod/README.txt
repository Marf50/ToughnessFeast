ToughnessFeast
==============

INVENTORY
  Open a character's inventory. A "Feast" button sits at the top-left.
  Click it for the limb / food journal (close with X).
  The vanilla Limbs button also opens this panel.

HUNGER HOVER
  Same journal on the Hunger tooltip.

  Feast          Hiver  toughness 23
  Food           248 / 300
  Left Arm       GONE  bud 34%  ~18 min
  Right Arm      62%  healing

Limbs only regrow when food is over 200.

If the Feast button crashes the game, set EnableInventoryMenu=0 in config.ini.

Extract this folder into Kenshi/mods/. Needs RE_Kenshi.
